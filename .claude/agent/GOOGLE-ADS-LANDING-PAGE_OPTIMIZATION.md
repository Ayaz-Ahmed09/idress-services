Landing Page Best Practices (Built into every service page)

GOOGLE ADS QUALITY SCORE FACTORS:

1. RELEVANCE:
   - H1 matches potential ad headline
   - Page content matches ad keywords
   - Service-specific pages for each ad group
   - Clear, specific sub-service descriptions

2. LANDING PAGE EXPERIENCE:
   - Fast page load (target < 2s on mobile)
   - Mobile-friendly responsive design
   - Easy navigation back to homepage
   - Clear business identity (name, address, phone)
   - Trust signals visible above fold
   - Privacy policy link accessible

3. CONVERSION OPTIMIZATION:
   - Multiple CTAs above fold (Call, WhatsApp, Book)
   - Phone number visible on every viewport
   - WhatsApp as alternative conversion path
   - Simple booking form (minimal fields)
   - Social proof (reviews, ratings, badges)
   - Urgency elements (24/7 available, same-day)
   - Pricing transparency (starting prices shown)
   - Clear service area information

4. AD-SPECIFIC FEATURES:
   - Each service page can serve as a dedicated landing page
   - URL structure clean for ad destination URLs
   - No redirect chains
   - Fast Time to Interactive
   - No intrusive interstitials
   - Content above fold matches ad promise

5. TRACKING READINESS:
   - Phone numbers wrapped in trackable elements
   - WhatsApp clicks trackable
   - Form submissions trackable
   - Google Ads conversion tracking ready
   - GA4 events ready for CTA clicks
